# How to play

- Let good code reach release and defeat bad code. You go bankrupt when funds are {bankrupt} {zeroFunds}
- Hold the mouse button to walk there. Click the hero to attack bad code within {range}px
- Keyboard: arrows or WASD move, Space attacks. Pad (bottom right): {up}{down}{left}{right} move, centre {attack} attacks
- Setup: pick a rank, click a coloured cell (start with 1 or more). Pick an item, click ({points}pt). × undo. "{start}"
- Click a spawn point: raise / cut, vacation / return, dismiss, AI agents
- Vacation: bad code -{vacationReduction}pts each, max -{vacationMax}, min {vacationMin}%. Lasts after return. Salary paid; no code on leave
- Engineer icon: hire (max {maxEngineers}). Salaries are paid at month end
- AI agents: faster output; cheaper ones mix in more bad code. Paid monthly
- Item icon: buy with funds; placed at the hero. The level rises every {levelUpDays} days and unlocks more items
- Balance icon: gauge left = features (sales), right = refactoring (repays debt). {refactoring} {feature} move {step}%
- {daysIcon} days {fundsIcon} funds {salesIcon} total sales {paymentIcon} next month-end payment
- Tech debt: {normal}{normalMin}-{normalMax} {caution}{cautionMin}-{cautionMax} {critical}{criticalMin}-{criticalMax}. Over {outage} for {outageDays} days: major outage
